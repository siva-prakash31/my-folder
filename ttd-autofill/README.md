# TTD Special Entry Darshan Auto-Fill Extension

A simple JavaScript Chrome extension designed to automatically fill in pilgrim details when booking Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) Special Entry Darshan tickets.

---

## Prerequisites

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Make sure to configure your pilgrim details in `content.js` before opening the TTD website.

1. Open `content.js` in a text editor.
2. Enter your pilgrim details and contact details according to the template provided in the file.
3. Save the file.

---

## Installation & Setup

Follow these steps to load the extension in Google Chrome:

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/` (or go to **Menu** > **Extensions** > **Manage Extensions**).
3. Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select the `ttd-autofill` folder from your file manager.

---

## How to Use

1. Navigate to the official [TTD booking portal](https://ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in/).
2. Proceed through the booking process until you reach the **Pilgrim Details** page.
3. You will see a **Fill Details** button injected onto the page.
4. Click **Fill Details** to automatically populate the pilgrim form fields.
5. Review the entered data and proceed to checkout.
